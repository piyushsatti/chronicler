# Requirements — Player characters are owner-mutated and staff-reviewed

System: [characters.md](/docs/systems/characters.md)

## Requirements

| #   | Requirement                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Because                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| R1  | A character has exactly one owner, `characters.owner_id`, set once at creation and checked in code on every write.                                                                                                                                                                 | It is never an editable table row. [`D14`](/docs/decisions/D14-Ownership-Is-Code-Override-Is-Declared.md)                                                                                                                                                                       |
| R2  | No principal besides the owning player may mutate a player character.                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Admins and verifiers may inspect it and record verification decisions, but may not edit it or initiate D&D Beyond sync. This is the explicit player-character exception to [`D29`](/docs/decisions/D29-Owner-And-Admin-Bypass-Identity-Predicates.md), owner ruling 2026-09-16. |
| R3  | The owner-only boundary covers the whole aggregate: profile/guild/status writes, progression and logs, verification submission, downtime, and D&D Beyond re-sync.                                                                                                                  | Distilled from system Verdict.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| R4  | Whether a character is trusted gates exactly one thing: quest signup.                                                                                                                                                                                                              | A verifier's approval decision is the source of that trust. `/admin/review` and the verifier's `/verify` tab show the same review queue (#20); route existence and MSW responses do not prove native backend decisions.                                                         |
| R5  | Character is one of ten aggregate roots. Its progression ledger, D&D Beyond mirror, downtime projects, and session logs commit inside the same boundary; nothing outside it touches those tables directly. [`D26`](/apps/api/docs/decisions/D26-Ten-Roots-Not-Forty-Five-Repos.md) | Distilled from system Verdict.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| R6  | A character requiring approval must carry a resolvable D&D Beyond link before approval.                                                                                                                                                                                            | The admission point, meaning/freshness of "resolves," and treatment of existing linkless characters remain explicit product decisions; a non-null or allowlisted URL alone is not proof of resolution.                                                                          |
| R7  | A failed D&D Beyond sync survives the immediate response and is visible later to the owner and the reviewing verifier.                                                                                                                                                             | Preserve a safe failure reason and attempt time/provenance; persistence shape, retry policy and retention are unresolved. A failed fetch must not replace a valid mirror with partial/null data.                                                                                |
| R8  | Payload drift fails visibly against dated, provenance-recorded D&D Beyond response fixtures.                                                                                                                                                                                       | Hand-authored fixtures prove parser consistency only. Capture authority, freshness policy and any live/scheduled probe are unresolved; fixture age or shape mismatch must not be reported as live parity.                                                                       |

## Verification recovery boundary

The September 16 owner-only amendment remains authoritative: owners initiate sync and character changes; staff inspect and record decisions, with no sync/edit action in the review queue. [Addressed notifications](/docs/interfaces/notifications.md) governs owner feedback from a decision.

Current source has `AdminReviewPage`, `CharacterVerifyTab` and `useCharacterVerifyTab`, including approve/change-request controls and an expected submission timestamp. Their REST queue/decision paths exist in field routing and feature-local MSW, but the Go character read slice does not mount those commands or a DDB sync/parser. `character_ddb_mirrors` stores payload/sync time, not persistent failure state. The consolidated schema has no character DDB URL field; the GraphQL/web views still expose one. R6–R8 and native decision behavior therefore remain unfinished; this documentation does not select a new public contract or persistence design.

Remaining owner choices: gate at submission or approval; define successful resolution and maximum evidence age; decide legacy linkless admission/backfill; choose failure persistence/retention and retry behavior; authorize fixture capture and choose manual/scheduled/CI drift checks. The verifier UI's former "no frontend page" verdict is superseded by actual current source, while end-to-end functionality remains unverified.

## Assumptions

| #   | Assumption                                                                                                                                                                                                    | If wrong                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A1  | Every character write that checks ownership uses one of [`D14`](/docs/decisions/D14-Ownership-Is-Code-Override-Is-Declared.md)'s declared breadths, so an unnamed check is the exception rather than the rule | The two live defects here are not isolated; other character writes carry the same silent over-permission, undetected until the next survey                                       |
| A2  | A four-value `character_status` enum with no `rejected` state is acceptable, so a rejection is recoverable only by reading the `verifications` ledger                                                         | A rejected character is indistinguishable from a never-reviewed one to anything reading `characters.status` alone, and a submitter has no status to check beyond "still pending" |
| A3  | A recovered DDB integration retains an explicit, revalidated SSRF-safe host/redirect policy.                                                                                                                  | The historical `ALLOWED_HOSTS` design is not evidence of a Go integration or current DDB host validity.                                                                          |
| A4  | The historical "any DM may sync/adjust XP" findings describe the surveyed legacy implementation.                                                                                                              | They do not authorize staff mutation and are not a current Go runtime verdict; R2/R3 and the September 16 amendment supersede that breadth.                                      |
