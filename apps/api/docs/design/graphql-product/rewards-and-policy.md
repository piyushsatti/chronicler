## Accepted reward-total coverage ruling, 2026-10-16

This is the final field disposition. `QuestSummaryTotalsV2Type.itemGrants` is retired because retained items have no accepted item-to-quest provenance and closure accepts PCXP only. Historical items are not thereby classified as cosmetic.
